world-countries-110m.json: the world's countries as a GeoJSON FeatureCollection, for the ChoroplethMap docs.

Source: Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 countries (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), version 4.1.0, as
redistributed in the world-atlas npm package (countries-110m.json, TopoJSON). Natural Earth data is in the
public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

Converted to GeoJSON with topojson-client, coordinates rounded to 2 decimals (kept as they were for the
7 small countries whose area rounding changed by more than 5 %), properties reduced to "name"; the feature
id is the ISO 3166-1 numeric code.
